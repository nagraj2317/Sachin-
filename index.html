Terms & Conditions

Version 1 · Last updated 12 October 2026 · Please read carefully before registering.

1. Organizer and contact

This competition is organized by RABHA ("Organizer"). Contact: Instagram @rabha_automation. Dates and updates are announced there and on this website.

2. Eligibility and entries

  • Students and other individuals may participate, solo or in a team of up to 5 people.
  • One person may be part of only one entry. Submitting more than one entry (solo and team, or several projects) leads to disqualification without refund.
  • Participants under 18 must have the consent of a parent or guardian.
  • All information you provide must be true.

3. Registration fee

Fee by entry size: 1 person ₹149, 2 persons ₹249, 3 persons ₹349, 4 persons ₹399, 5 persons ₹499. Registration is complete only after the Organizer verifies your payment (UTR number and payment screenshot). Paid fees are not refundable, except as stated in sections 4 and 5.

4. Cancellation, postponement and refund

  • If the Organizer cancels the competition, or cannot hold it for any reason, registration fees will be refunded after deducting unavoidable costs already spent (payment gateway charges, platform and promotion costs and any referral commission earned). The deductions will be shown to participants and refunds made within 30 days.
  • If the competition is postponed, your registration stays valid for the new dates.
  • Duplicate or wrong payments will be refunded after verification.
  • If the Organizer removes an entry because the idea is illegal, harmful, unsuitable or against these terms, the entry will be deleted and the registration fee will be refunded to the payer. This does not apply to fraud, fake payment proof or plagiarism (see sections 6 and 8).
  • If the competition has to be stopped for any reason, the fee will be refunded after a deduction of unavoidable costs, as described above. The deducted amount will be told to you.

5. How the competition works

  • Participants submit a project idea. Ideas are shown on this website and rated by the community and by the Organizer.
  • The top ideas are shortlisted. Selected teams get 10 days to build their project and submit a working demo or link and a pitch deck (PDF or link). Photos and videos are optional.
  • The Organizer tests the submissions and announces winners. The Organizer's decision is final.
  • Selected teams that miss the deadline may be replaced by another team.

6. Use of AI and originality

You may use AI tools, but you must honestly disclose where and how much you used them. Your idea and work must be your own and must not copy another person's work. Participants may be asked to explain their code and project live. Undisclosed AI use, plagiarism or inability to explain the work can lead to disqualification.

7. Prizes

The cash prize pool depends on the number of verified registrations and will be announced before registration closes. Gadget prizes are given only if officially announced. Prizes are given to the winning entry; a team shares the prize among its members itself, and the Organizer is not responsible for that division. Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes.

8. Ratings and fair play

Fake accounts, vote manipulation, fake payment proof, harassment or any fraud lead to disqualification and loss of any prize or refund. You cannot rate your own idea.

9. Photos, videos, pitch decks and personal information

  • Do not include personal information (names, mobile numbers, email addresses, photos of faces, social media handles) in your photos, videos, pitch deck or idea text. Do not include your project's source code. Show only how the idea or project works, using a normal pitch deck or PDF.
  • Photos, videos and pitch decks are shown blurred to the public until the Organizer has checked them. Phone numbers and email addresses typed into public text are hidden automatically. This is a safety measure and does not replace your own responsibility.
  • The Organizer may hide, blur, edit or remove any content that contains personal information, source code, copyrighted material you do not own, or anything illegal, abusive or misleading.
  • You are responsible for everything you upload or link.

10. Ownership of work

You keep ownership of your idea and project. By participating you allow the Organizer to display your idea title, description, links and winner details on the website and social media for promotion.

11. Referral creators

  • Creators receive a personal referral link. A registration counts for a creator only when it was made through that link and the Organizer has verified its payment.
  • Commission is calculated on the fees of verified paid registrations: no commission below 10 verified registrations, 8% from 10 up to 100, and 10% above 100.
  • Commission is paid after registration closes. Fake, self-made or duplicate registrations are not counted, and the Organizer may cancel a creator's commission for misuse.
  • Creators see only counts and amounts, never participants' personal details, and must not misuse participants' information.

12. Limitation of liability

The competition is provided as is. The Organizer is not liable for technical problems, internet or payment-service failures, or indirect losses. Our total responsibility is limited to the registration fee you paid.

13. Changes and governing law

The Organizer may update these terms, dates, or rules; important changes will be announced. Continuing to participate means you accept the updated terms. These terms are governed by the laws of India.

Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Last updated 12 October 2026 · This explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account and register we collect: name, email, password (stored securely by Google Firebase, we cannot read it), WhatsApp number, city, school or college and course details, what you do, profile photo link (optional), team member details, your project details and links, payment UTR number, a screenshot of your payment (visible only to the Organizer), and which referral link you used. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create your account and registration, and to verify your payment.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp or email about the competition, results and prizes.
  • To show ideas, run ratings and select winners.
  • To calculate referral commission for creators.

3. Who can see your information

  • Public: your idea title, summary and project details, ratings, and your photos, videos and pitch deck (blurred until the Organizer has checked them).
  • Private: your name, phone, email, payment details and personal profile are visible only to the Organizer.
  • Referral creators only see counts and amounts; they do not see participants' personal details.
  • We do not sell your data. We share it only if required by law.

4. Protecting personal data in public content

Please do not put personal information or source code in your photos, videos, pitch deck or idea text. Phone numbers and email addresses written in public text are hidden automatically, and uploaded media stays blurred until the Organizer confirms it contains no personal information. If something personal is visible by mistake, contact us and we will remove it.

5. Cookies and local storage

  • Essential: we keep you logged in (Firebase sign-in) and remember the referral code from the link you opened. The site cannot work without these.
  • Your choice: your cookie choice is saved on your device. If you accept all and analytics is switched on, we use Google Analytics to count visits. If you choose essential only, no analytics is loaded.

6. Storage and security

Data is stored with Google Firebase. We use access rules so that private details are not public, but no online system is completely risk-free.

7. Children

If you are under 18, register only with the consent of a parent or guardian. Parents may contact us to review or delete a child's information.

8. Retention and your rights

We keep data while the competition and its payments, results and disputes are active, and for a reasonable period after. You can ask to correct or delete your data by contacting @rabha_automation on Instagram; some payment records may need to be kept.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here.
